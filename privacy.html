Privacy policy

Personal Dashboard · last updated 14 October 2026

Who uses this application

This is a private, single-user dashboard that runs only on its owner's own computer. It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.

What data it reads

  • Google Calendar: the owner's events, to show them and to create, edit or delete events the owner chooses to change from the dashboard.
  • Gmail: the owner's inbox, to show recent unread messages, and sending email only to the owner's own address (a password-reset code).
  • Notion and Spotify: the pages and playback the owner connects.
  • Bank accounts, through Enable Banking and only with the owner's consent given at their bank: balances and transactions, read-only. It cannot make payments or transfers.

Where the data is kept

  • Everything is stored locally on the owner's computer. Data is only exchanged with the services listed above, which is needed to read it.
  • No data is sold, shared, used for advertising, used to train AI models or analysed by third parties.
  • The financial section is protected by a password chosen by the owner.

Google user data

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to show it to the owner and to carry out the changes the owner asks for.

Removing the data and the access

The owner can disconnect any service at any time from the dashboard. Google access can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The bank access can be revoked at the bank or in Enable Banking, and it expires by itself after at most 180 days.
